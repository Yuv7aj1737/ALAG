FAQ

Straight answers.

What exactly does this do?
It joins Minecraft servers with bot accounts and advertises for you in chat — on rotation, through proxies, 24/7. When someone types your trigger keyword, the bot DMs them your pitch automatically.
Do I need to download anything?
No. The bots run on our infrastructure. You control everything from the web dashboard after logging in with Discord.
Which servers does it work on?
Big PvP networks like MCPVP, CatPvP and Minemen are one-click presets, but you can point it at any server IP. SRV records and version detection are handled for you.
How do I add accounts?
Paste a session ID (SSID). The bot pulls the username from it and joins. No passwords ever touch the dashboard.
What happens when a bot gets kicked or banned?
You'll see the exact reason in the console. Region transfers and normal kicks are followed automatically. If an account gets banned, it stops retrying instead of digging the hole deeper — swap in another session ID and keep going.
Can I see the dashboard before buying?
Yeah. Log in with Discord and you can look around the whole dashboard — it stays view-only until you redeem a key.
